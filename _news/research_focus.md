Excited to announce that I've started my PhD in Computer Science at the **University of Virginia**!

I'll be working at the **Collaborative Robotics and Autonomous Learning (CRΔL)** lab under the guidance of [Prof. Rohan Chandra](https://cral-uva.github.io/people/rohan_chandra/index.html).

My research will focus on closing the sim-to-real gap using 3D Gaussian-based digital twins, with emphasis on intelligent dynamic agents, physics-based object interactions, and scalable data generation for robotics applications.

Looking forward to this new chapter and exciting research ahead!
